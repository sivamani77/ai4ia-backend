I want us to take a look at the importance of representation of languages.
Language is more than just a tool of communication.
It is a vessel of identity, of knowledge and of memory.
Yet many of our artificial intelligence systems today privilege a small number of dominant
global languages.
If African languages such as CISWATI are excluded from artificial intelligence models, our people
are excluded from the future of digital knowledge.
This not only risks marginalization, but also the erosion of intangible cultural heritage
carried through our words, our proverbs, our oral traditions.
UNESCO has repeatedly emphasized multilingualism as a pillar of information accessibility.
For Eswatini and for many nations in Africa,
investing in artificial intelligence that understands and respects local languages
is not just a technology, is not just a technological necessity.
It is a cultural imperative.
I also want to get into demographics.
AI cannot be neutral if the data it learns from is incomplete.
If algorithms are trained primarily on data from certain regions, populations or socio-economic groups,
the resulting technologies will inevitably reflect those biases.
And importantly, it is crucial that these data comes from our nations, our stories.
In Africa, where over 60% of the population is under the age of 25, the demographic realities
are unique.
For artificial intelligence to be relevant to us, it must be shaped by the youth voices
and serve youth needs in education, employment, health and innovation.
Equally important is the inclusion of women, rural communities and persons with disabilities.
These groups must not remain on the margins of digital development.
If they are invisible in the AI data, they will remain invisible in AI solutions.
Bias in, bias out.
Representation here is not a matter of fairness alone.
It is a matter of ensuring the effectiveness and sustainability of artificial intelligence.
It is important that we exercise inclusion when we exercise digital advancement
to ensure that everyone is included in the solutions with precision.
